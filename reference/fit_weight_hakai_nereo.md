# Hakai Institute Nereocystis weight fit

A fit of
[`kelpbio::kb_fit_weight_nereo()`](https://hakaiinstitute.github.io/kelpbio/reference/kb_fit_weight_nereo.html)
to plants harvested by the Hakai Institute in British Columbia after day
of year 140, measured at the maximum sub-bulb diameter.

## Usage

``` r
fit_weight_hakai_nereo
```

## Format

An object of class
`c("kb_fit_weight_nereo", "kb_fit_weight", "kb_fit")`. The data are in
`fit$data`.
